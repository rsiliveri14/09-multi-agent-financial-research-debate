# Demo script (~90 seconds)

In the UI: **Play 90s demo**, or follow the clicks below.

1. Open the debate console. Show empty state and live `/health`.
   Screenshot: `docs/screenshots/01-empty-floor.png`
2. Run: *What were Apple total net sales in FY2024, and did Greater China grow?*
   Screenshot: `docs/screenshots/02-apple-debate.png`
3. Point at Bull (consolidated growth) vs Bear (China decline) vs Financial (numbers).
4. Open the disagreement matrix — at least one `disagree` cell.
   Screenshot: `docs/screenshots/03-disagreement-matrix.png`
5. Switch to Graph: claims cite filing pages; contradictions are red edges.
   Screenshot: `docs/screenshots/04-provenance-graph.png`
6. Run the 8-K vs 10-Q Apple question. Show unresolved or 10-Q preference, not fake consensus.
   Screenshot: `docs/screenshots/05-unresolved-8k-10q.png`
7. Open Eval and compare single-agent RAG vs debate (admin).
   Screenshot: `docs/screenshots/06-eval-comparison.png`
8. State limitations: synthetic corpus, heuristic provider by default, not investment advice.

A committed MP4 is optional; the playable in-app demo plus these stills are the portfolio artifact.
